# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

OpenWave IPTV is a community-driven project built around collaboration, accessibility, and the responsible sharing of information.

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone who participates in the project, regardless of their background, identity, experience, or level of technical knowledge.

We believe that constructive collaboration makes the project better for everyone.

## Our Standards

We expect all contributors, maintainers, and community members to help maintain a positive and respectful environment.

### Expected Behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contribute to a healthy community include:

- Being respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them understand the project.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Providing feedback that focuses on the work rather than the individual.
- Respecting different opinions, experiences, and approaches.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Collaborating in good faith.
- Respecting copyright, licensing, and third-party rights.

### Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not tolerated:

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or hateful language.
- Threats, violent language, or encouragement of harm.
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or contributions.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately antagonising other contributors.
- Repeatedly ignoring project guidelines or maintainer instructions.
- Knowingly submitting malicious code, harmful links, or unauthorised content.

Disagreements are acceptable. Personal attacks and hostile behaviour are not.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces associated with OpenWave IPTV, including:

- GitHub repositories.
- Issues and pull requests.
- GitHub Discussions, where available.
- Project-related communication channels.
- Any other space where an individual is officially representing the project.

It applies to both online and offline interactions related to the project.

## Responsibilities of Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards outlined in this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, commits, and other content that violates these standards.

They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation from individuals whose behaviour is deemed inappropriate.

Maintainers are expected to apply these standards fairly and consistently.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it to the project maintainers through an appropriate private communication channel.

When reporting an incident, please include:

- A description of the incident.
- The relevant GitHub username or individuals involved, if known.
- Links or screenshots providing context, where available.
- Any additional information that may help the maintainers understand the situation.

Reports will be handled as discreetly as reasonably possible.

Please do not use public Issues or Discussions to expose private information or escalate personal disputes.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reported incidents and determine an appropriate response based on the circumstances and severity of the behaviour.

Possible actions include:

1. **Warning** — A private or public warning requesting that the behaviour stop.
2. **Temporary restriction** — A temporary restriction on participation in project spaces.
3. **Removal of contributions** — Removal of inappropriate comments, submissions, or other content.
4. **Permanent exclusion** — Permanent removal of participation privileges from the project.

Maintainers reserve the right to take immediate action in cases involving serious misconduct, threats, harassment, or malicious activity.
